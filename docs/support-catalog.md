# First-wave CLI support catalog — offline baseline

Transcribed from the iteration 01 design catalog; evidence links point at the checked-in copies.

## Catalog contract

FP-7 covers this entire document. Captured **2026-09-25, Linux amd64**. `VERIFIED` means supported by the named local help/version output or installed-file excerpt; it does **not** mean a model invocation succeeded. `UNVERIFIED` means unavailable from that evidence and must not be promoted to a runtime guarantee. No model, login, models-list, MCP-connect or network command was run. `--help` and `--version` ran only. Codex printed a read-only PATH-alias warning and still returned help/version successfully; that warning is not evidence that worker execution would succeed.

All commands below are illustrative argv contracts, not commands run during this job. `<model>`, `<effort>`, `<prompt>`, `<plane-url>`, `<ca-path>` and `<runbook>` are placeholders, never factory-default model choices. Adapter selection was first fixed by iteration 08 for Claude/Codex and 11 for Grok/Cursor; the current policy is the [worker selection policy](#worker-selection-policy) below: Callsheet never silently drops an effort, and the vendor, not Callsheet, decides whether a valid model/effort pair runs. Do not pass `--resume`, `--continue`, vendor worktree flags, cloud-worker mode or vendor MCP-server mode for task workers.

**Iteration 08 runtime evidence (Claude and Codex workers).** The owner's Linux qualification captures of **2026-09-30** (Linux 6.8.0 x86_64, `claude` 2.1.285, `codex` 0.159.0) are checked in byte-for-byte under [`tests/testdata/real-adapters/linux-2026-09-30/`](../tests/testdata/real-adapters/linux-2026-09-30/) with a provenance [manifest](../tests/testdata/real-adapters/linux-2026-09-30/manifest.json) (destination, source name, bytes and SHA-256 of every file; absent capture files listed as absent). Unlike the help captures above, these runs did call models: exact argv, stdin, working directory, stdout, stderr, exit and final-file bytes of successful and failed headless runs, and baseline/candidate write canaries. They supersede the help-level worker candidates for Claude and Codex only; the older help captures (Claude 2.1.282, Codex 0.156.1) remain the evidence for the coordinator facts and are not relabeled as captures of the newer versions. The Claude and Codex worker adapters and their operator procedure are described in [real adapters](real-adapters.md).

The baseline has intentionally incomplete runtime facts. Record worker launch recipe, model, effort, approval suppression, sandbox effect for Linux/macOS, final-message extraction and exit behavior; coordinator stdio config, call timeout, override, progress extension and runbook loading, even when `UNVERIFIED`. A help claim and its unverified runtime consequences are separate fields.

The machine-readable snapshot of this catalog is [`tests/testdata/support-catalog.json`](../tests/testdata/support-catalog.json): four entries (`id`, `version`, `coordinator_version`, `platform`, `facts`, in that canonical order), each with the thirteen facts `headless`, `model`, `effort`, `approval`, `sandbox_linux`, `sandbox_macos`, `final_message`, `exit_codes`, `mcp_config`, `mcp_timeout`, `mcp_timeout_override`, `mcp_progress_extension` and `runbook`. Every fact is `VERIFIED` or `UNVERIFIED` with a value, repository-root-relative evidence paths and the verifying iteration, one per-key rule (`catalog.Owner`): `07b` for the three coordinator timeout facts `mcp_timeout`, `mcp_timeout_override` and `mcp_progress_extension` of every vendor (iteration 07b's local timeout qualification), and otherwise `08` for Claude/Codex and `11` for Grok/Cursor, including `mcp_config`. Mixed facts are wholly `UNVERIFIED`, with the verified subset described in the value. The captured help/version/excerpt evidence is checked in under [`tests/testdata/cli-help/`](../tests/testdata/cli-help/); `TestFP7CatalogContract` validates structure and completeness, not vendor behaviour. Each entry carries two separate identities (design catalog-version). The **Worker version** (`version`) is the exact historical worker observation that the worker facts describe (iteration 08 for Claude/Codex, 11 for Grok/Cursor) and, since design 12a-worker-selection, the worker's minimum version baseline: a worker executable is eligible at or above it, and it is neither the installed version nor an exact-only execution constraint. Its presence is not itself permission to execute a worker (Cursor's execution is refused outright). The **MCP publication version** (`coordinator_version`) is the exact CLI `--version` identity that new MCP timeout publication targets: `mcpqual` publishes a run only when its observed version equals it byte for byte (no normalization or semver matching), and a missing or different observation is a publication conflict (exit 4). It is not a worker qualification and not a claim that every coordinator feature is qualified. It is mandatory: a missing, empty or whitespace-only `coordinator_version` fails validation as `entry <i> (<id>): missing coordinator_version`, and publication refuses such a catalog as invalid input (exit 2) before any session. There is no fallback to `version` and no runtime migration; old binaries reject the new member, so the schema and the publisher ship together. The two may be equal (Grok, Cursor) or differ (Claude, Codex, whose installed CLIs are newer than their worker-qualified versions). Historical help captures still describe their captured versions: in particular `runbook` and the historical part of `mcp_config` are not re-certified by `coordinator_version`, and each new `mcp_config` observation names its own qualification run and version through that run's report. Publication rewrites exactly the three timeout bullets of a vendor's section in this document; the JSON also receives the run's initialize `clientInfo` observation in `mcp_config`, which this document does not repeat (read it in the linked qualification report), and the older registration prose below is never rewritten by it.

## Worker selection policy

Current policy (design 12a-worker-selection, requirement Q12); the vendor worker facts above remain the historical observations. A role or dispatch override names an explicit, required model: free text (1 to 1024 bytes of UTF-8 without control characters) passed unchanged, with no model list kept and no model inferred or defaulted. The effort must be one of the adapter's efforts: claude `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`, `max` (its captured `--effort` help); codex `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`, `max`, `ultra` (the coordinator's observation of the installed Codex 0.160.0 model cache on 2026-10-10 and the owner's decision of that date, approving the union rather than a per-model table; no committed capture); grok `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh` (its captured accepted-effort list); cursor `none`, `minimal`, `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`, `max` (its listed model variants, Extra High as `xhigh`). Callsheet refuses an invalid model or an effort outside that set before anything runs; a valid but unknown model or unsupported pair reaches the vendor, whose refusal is the task's result. The observed pairs above are evidence, not an allowlist or a default.

Worker versions are eligible at or above the catalog `version`, the minimum, without a version-drift warning: Claude `SEMVER (Claude Code)`, Codex `codex-cli SEMVER` and Grok `grok SEMVER (HASH) [CHANNEL]` order by SemVer precedence (Grok's hash and channel never order), and Cursor `YYYY.MM.DD-HASH` by its calendar date, a same-date version with another hash being equal and accepted. An older, malformed or foreign version line is refused. This is an eligibility policy, not authentication and not a qualification of later releases; the exact `coordinator_version` publication identity is unaffected. The operator procedure and the error texts are in [real adapters](real-adapters.md#selection-and-versions).
